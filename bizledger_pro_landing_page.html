Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Bizledger Pro ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and use our mobile application (Package ID: com.aistudio.bizledger.kfrx).

2. Local Data Storage & Offline Architecture

Bizledger Pro is engineered as an offline-first financial tool. All financial records, transactions, invoices, vendor bills, and company setups are stored securely and exclusively on your local device. We do not operate proprietary backend servers that capture or sync your financial database.

3. No Third-Party Data Collection

We do not collect, share, rent, or sell any personal or financial data to third-party advertisers, analytics providers, or external entities. Your financial data never leaves your device unless you manually export a JSON backup or generate a PDF statement.

4. Backups and Exports

You retain absolute control over your data backups. The app allows you to export your data into standard JSON backup files or print/save statements locally. These files reside entirely within your device storage or your chosen local file destination.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, inquiries, or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact our developer support team directly at doublejjjonck@gmail.com.
